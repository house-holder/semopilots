Contact

About the author

I'm Kyle Householder: An A&P for over a decade, commercial pilot with single- and multi-engine land privileges, and an instrument-rated flight instructor.

I've worked as a widebody airline mechanic, depot-level heavy check mechanic, engine builder, and now I'm full-time instructing as I chase the career change.

I volunteer my time and money to maintain this site. I am not a direct representative of either SEMO State University, the Cape Girardeau Regional Airport, or US Aviation Academy and opinions here are my own.

That being said, I hope indirectly I can represent the positive in all of these organizations and be a good steward of general aviation at the same time!
